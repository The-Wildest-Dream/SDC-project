# Quotation Assistant — motion film

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Show how a small manufacturer can turn scattered quote inputs into a traceable, human-reviewed price decision.
- **Identity:** A warm ivory drafting surface, ink-navy machinery, and vermilion decision marks; monumental condensed display type against precise mono annotations.
- **Hero:** A quotation sheet that is progressively built from cost blocks, historical price signals, and a versioned approval record.
- **Motion:** Precision paper folds, a radial comparison dial, and a camera-like push through the saved quote snapshot.
- **Arc:** Start with the friction of disconnected inputs; accelerate through calculation and evidence; decelerate at the human approval; resolve in a clean, durable record.
- **Payoff:** The final quote sheet folds into a single red registration mark, which becomes the opening's first mark.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 28 seconds.
- **Audience / takeaway:** Manufacturing sales teams and managers; transparent references support a human-owned quote and simulated approval, rather than making a decision for them.
- **Truth note:** The film describes the demo workflow. It does not imply production deployment, real customer data, optimal profit, or automated approval. Any graphic figures are explicitly marked illustrative.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 00.00–05.00s / 5.00s — “报价，不该靠拼凑。”
**Purpose:** Open on the real coordination problem without claiming a quantified business outcome.

**Exact content and holds:** At 00.20s, `报价` appears at 8.2vmin. At 00.72s, `不该靠` resolves below it at 8.2vmin. At 01.20s, the final `拼凑。` punches to 10vmin and holds to 04.55s. Small copy `成本 · 历史 · 账期 · 审批` appears at 02.15s and holds through 04.55s. At 03.60s, small mono qualifier `模拟演示 / 合成数据` appears in the lower-right safe zone.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Begin with three cropped ivory paper strips on a deep ink-navy desk: a cost label, a dated history fragment, and a payment-term slip. Their corners register like misaligned print plates. The headline owns the left 57%; slips occupy the right 43%, never crossing the text. Warm raking light, subtle paper grain, hairline vermilion registration marks. End with the strips precisely aligned into a quote folio, seen at a 7° angle.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45s: vermilion registration dot snaps into center-right; three paper strips pop in 0.45s apart with stiff, discrete motion. 0.45–1.35s: each strip rotates a few degrees into registration as the phrase completes. 1.45–2.10s: tiny field labels punch on one by one. 2.20–3.25s: all slips converge into a single folio; its corner creases with a masked fold. 3.25–4.40s: camera pushes toward the folio corner as a vermilion line traces its edge. 4.40–5.00s: the line remains in transit and extends into the next shot.

**Transition contract:** The outgoing fold line becomes the vertical cost-allocation bracket in Shot 02; combine the continuing line with a 3-panel paper split and slight camera push, not a generic wipe.

**Assets and technique:** CSS paper geometry, SVG registration marks and fold-line; locally loaded Chinese-capable Noto Sans SC + IBM Plex Mono. No existing image asset.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental electronic-percussive bed; opening dot and paper snaps should sit on musical accents. Alignment pending generated audio.

### Shot 02 / 05.00–10.00s / 5.00s — “先把成本摊清。”
**Purpose:** Explain the product's transparent cost-snapshot and quotation-floor role.

**Exact content and holds:** `先把成本摊清。` enters at 05.25s at 7.7vmin and holds to 09.55s. Three ledger labels appear in sequence: `材料` at 05.65s, `人工` at 06.20s, `制造费用` at 06.75s. At 07.50s show `批次费用 ÷ 数量` and `物流 ÷ 数量`. At 08.25s show `成本快照 → 最低毛利率 → 报价参考`. Persistent lower edge: `演示规则，不替代人工判断`.

**Composition and artistic idea:** A panoramic ivory cost ledger occupies the full width, unlike the opener's skewed scraps. Four horizontal bands fill in from left to right, each in a different measured ink tint. The carried red fold line passes down the center and becomes the calculation spine. A red `底线` rule is visually distinct from a fine navy `目标` line; figures are abstract ruled marks, not fabricated prices.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35s: carried line lands as a bracket; camera pulls out to reveal full-width ledger. 0.35–1.40s: material, labor, and overhead bands populate one at a time; the quantity counter-like ticks divide batch and logistics into per-unit marks without showing a fake numeric total. 1.50–2.50s: a fine target line draws across the width. 2.50–3.40s: vermilion floor line draws beneath it; the labels separate into two distinct layers. 3.40–4.15s: the bands fold at the divider into radial ticks. 4.15–5.00s: camera punches through those ticks as the red line curves into a circular gauge.

**Transition contract:** Red floor line curls into the outer arc of Shot 03; overlay an unfolding ledger seam and a shallow focus push.

**Assets and technique:** Animated SVG rules and cost bands, paper fold via polygon clip-path, numeric glyph ticks. No fake business amounts.

**Sound intent:** Keep the music bed; sharper percussive accents on the three cost arrivals and red floor-rule draw. Alignment pending.

### Shot 03 / 10.00–16.10s / 6.10s — “参考，不是决定。”
**Purpose:** Distinguish comparable transaction statistics and historical-price prediction from a manually proposed price and the cost floor.

**Exact content and holds:** `参考，不是决定。` at 10.25s, 7.4vmin, holds until 15.60s. Inside the gauge, labels `可比历史` at 10.85s, `历史成交价预测` at 12.15s, `成本底线` at 13.25s arrive separately. At 14.20s show `人工拟报价` as the larger bright marker. Footer from 10.35s to 15.65s: `预测历史成交单价；不代表利润最优价或购买概率`.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Centered radial instrument, occupying roughly 54% of the short edge, with a compact upper-left text column. Historical comparable prices appear as six varied ticks that emerge from a dark arc, not as a fake chart series with unverified values. The model marker is a fine dotted ring outside the solid cost-floor ring, making their meanings materially different. The proposed quote appears as a hand-placed vermilion tab that does not snap automatically to either ring.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.55s: line from Shot 02 opens a circular aperture; ticks populate sequentially. 0.65–1.65s: the historical band sweeps through one arc and settles. 1.90–2.70s: dotted model ring draws separately, then pauses. 3.00–3.75s: a fine floor ring locks in place. 4.05–4.65s: a hand-placed vermilion proposal tab slides a short distance and stops between reference marks. 4.75–5.35s: typography resolves into the distinction line; 5.35–6.10s: aperture tightens into a quote-card window and begins an overlapping split reveal.

**Transition contract:** The red proposal tab carries into Shot 04 as a corner registration mark on a saved quote card; its aperture expands while the outgoing chart splits into narrow paper panels.

**Assets and technique:** SVG radial ticks and circles; data visual is schematic and explicitly non-quantitative. Scale and aperture reveal, not lateral slide.

**Sound intent:** Music accent under the completed model ring and again as the human proposal tab arrives. Alignment pending.

### Shot 04 / 16.10–22.20s / 6.10s — “人来拟价，人来审批。”
**Purpose:** Show manual quote ownership, versioned snapshots, and simulated manager approval, without implying automated or real-world governance.

**Exact content and holds:** `人来拟价` at 16.30s in 7.4vmin; `人来审批` replaces it at 18.80s. Both hold in their respective beats. A quote artifact carries `报价草稿 · V01` from 16.50s. At 18.30s it changes to `待模拟审批`; at 19.40s it receives a vermilion outline stamp `已批准 · 演示`. Bottom label from 16.35s to 21.95s: `报价版本快照 · 操作记录`.

**Composition and artistic idea:** A tall quotation card dominates the right two-thirds, tilted 3° against a pale green-gray field; the headline is in a narrow left column. The carried red registration tab locks to the card's top corner. The card's lower paper layer lifts to expose a second offset version beneath it; the manager mark lands as a dry-print outline rather than a UI button. The seal is deliberately marked `演示`.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45s: chart aperture opens into the quote card; it scales from 0.82 to 1.0 with a brief settle while paper panels unfold. 0.60–1.45s: editable-looking but noninteractive lines populate; the card is labeled draft. 1.65–2.35s: V01 peels back to reveal a staggered second snapshot, then returns to the top. 2.60–3.30s: heading changes from `人来拟价` to `人来审批`; pending label is stamped. 3.45–4.20s: outline approval mark presses into the card with an ink spread; `演示` stays legible. 4.30–5.20s: tiny audit marks trace one by one beneath the card. 5.20–6.10s: card folds inward, stamp mark becomes an archive seal and moves to center, maintaining momentum.

**Transition contract:** Stamp ring stays in the same position and scale as it becomes Shot 05's archive seal; combine a vertical card fold and a controlled camera pull-back.

**Assets and technique:** CSS paper layers, SVG outlined stamp and audit lines, motion typography. No real person's name or business record.

**Sound intent:** One restrained paper/ink-like accent at the simulated approval beat is represented by the music only; no separate SFX requested. Alignment pending.

### Shot 05 / 22.20–28.00s / 5.80s — “每个报价，都有来处。”
**Purpose:** Conclude on a traceable record, not a sales promise.

**Exact content and holds:** `每个报价` appears at 22.50s in 7.7vmin, then `都有来处。` completes at 23.35s. Hold to 27.30s. Under the seal, labels arrive sequentially: `成本快照` at 23.80s, `审批记录` at 24.55s, `CSV / 可打印报价单` at 25.30s. Final qualifier `SME MANUFACTURING QUOTATION ASSISTANT · 模拟演示` enters at 25.95s and holds through 27.55s.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Unlike the previous offset card, center a compact archive folio on a quiet ink-navy field with ample ivory negative space. The three record labels orbit once around the approval seal, then fold under its edge, becoming a single stacked document. A small plain-text project name sits below; no invented logo or CTA. The scene closes in an ivory field with one red registration dot in the exact opening position.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45s: the carried seal centers as camera pulls back; the palette flips from paper to ink. 0.50–1.40s: final phrase resolves with opposing masked lines. 1.65–2.85s: cost snapshot, approval record, and export labels arrive one by one, each connected by a thin trace line. 3.00–3.85s: lines fold beneath the folio; the seal settles as the sole focal mark. 4.00–4.70s: project name and demo qualifier resolve; the end lockup is readable. 4.70–5.20s: the archive folds into one small red registration dot, moving toward the opener's center-right origin. 5.20–5.80s: dot expands past the frame and exposes the opening scraps; no empty field between loops.

**Transition contract:** Use the archive seal's circle as the red registration dot; match its center and scale to Shot 01's first frame, combining the palette inversion with a fold-through and aperture expansion.

**Assets and technique:** SVG seal, vector rules, CSS folio; no invented brand mark. Use motion masks and transform/opacity choreography.

**Sound intent:** Music resolves on the last lockup and sustains into the loop point. Alignment pending.

## Variety and pacing review
Adjacent shots vary their field, framing, dominant mechanism, and subject scale: paper collage / panoramic ledger / radial instrument / angled record card / centered archive. Each shot includes at least three timed post-entrance events and hands off a physical geometry (fold line, cost-rule arc, proposal tab, approval seal, registration dot). No scene uses a full-frame texture plate more than once. The last fifth of each shot contains transfer motion. All price evidence is shown schematically with no implied real transaction values.
